01 — About

Engineer across the full stack of hardware & software

I'm a final-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo, graduating in May 2026. Over four co-op terms I've shipped work at Tesla, Motorola Solutions, Neutron Controls, and Accelerated Systems Inc. — spanning industrial controls, embedded firmware, cybersecurity tooling, and PCB hardware design.

My sweet spot is taking a system from model to firmware to bring-up: tuning control loops in MATLAB/Simulink, writing the embedded C/C++ that runs them, designing the boards in Altium, and building the software that monitors it all. I like hard, multidisciplinary problems — self-balancing robots, power electronics, real-time telemetry, and AI tooling.
